Guest Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reserving an evening, our seasonal hearth formats, dietary accommodations, and estate policies.

How far in advance do reservations open?

Reservations for our dining room open on the 1st of every calendar month at 9:00 AM PST for the following month. For members of our Supper Club (Earth Tender and Hearth Steward tiers), exclusive advance priority booking opens 7 days earlier with dedicated concierge support.

Can you accommodate allergies and strict dietary preferences?

Yes, with advance notice. We provide dedicated menu adaptations for vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, celiac / gluten-free, and nut allergies. Because our ingredients are harvested from our beds and surrounding collective farms daily, please specify all dietary requirements in your booking notes at least 48 hours prior to your seating.

What is your cancellation and deposit policy?

Tasting reservations may be cancelled or rescheduled up to 48 hours before your seating without charge. Cancellations within 48 hours incur a $50 per person fee to cover ingredients already harvested specifically for your party. Supper Club communal banquets are non-refundable but are fully transferable to another guest.

What is the dress code and arrival protocol?

We embrace Farmstead Smart Casual. We encourage comfortable footwear if you wish to join our optional 30-minute pre-dinner orchard walk through the biodynamic gardens at 17:15. Evening temperatures in the valley can drop after sunset, so light layering is recommended for hearth terrace seating.

Are full estate buyouts and private weddings available?

Yes. Our 20-acre estate, hearth dining room, and covered glasshouse pavilion are available for private celebrations, intimate weddings, and corporate gatherings of up to 60 seated guests. Buyouts feature custom multi-course menus crafted alongside Chef Arlo Vance and curated cellar pairings. Inquire via the form above or email events@harvestandhearth.com.

Can children and families dine at Harvest & Hearth?

Due to our multi-course tasting format which typically spans 2.5 to 3 hours with active wood-fire hearth operations, evening dinner services are best suited for adults and youth aged 12 and older. We warmly welcome families with young children to our seasonal Sunday Afternoon Farm Table brunches held from May through October.
