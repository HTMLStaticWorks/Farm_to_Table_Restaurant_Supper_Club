Dietary Integrity & Care

Tailored to Your Health & Philosophy

Because we prepare every single course from raw, whole ingredients harvested that morning, our culinary brigade can comfortably accommodate celiac, vegan, dairy-free, and complex allergy requirements with 24 hours notice.

  • Dedicated gluten-free hearth cookware and clean prep station.
  • 100% seed-oil free: we cook solely with cold-pressed olive oil, sunflower oil, or lard.
  • Zero artificial thickeners, gums, or refined white sugars.
