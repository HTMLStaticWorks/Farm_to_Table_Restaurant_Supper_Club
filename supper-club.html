The Communal Gathering

The Harvest & Hearth Supper Club

An unhurried 24-seat feast where strangers gather as neighbors, local growers break bread with diners, and cellar wines flow until midnight.

Alternate Saturday Evenings • 7:00 PM Prompt

A Return to the Ancient Ritual of Feasting

In an era of rushed meals and digital distraction, the Supper Club is our intentional antidote. We seat twenty-four guests along a single 40-foot hand-hewn oak table carved from estate timber.

There is no table turnover. No menu choices to debate. You arrive at dusk, receive a wild botanical aperitif by the outdoor hearth fire, and take your seat for an eight-course shared banquet celebrating that week's harvest pinnacle.

The Experience Elements

What Makes Our Evenings Unforgettable

Four guiding rituals that distinguish our Supper Club from traditional restaurant reservations.

Hearthside Welcome

Arrive at 7:00 PM for outdoor fireside botanical elixirs, wood-roasted appetizers, and introductions under orchard trees.

8-Course Shared Feast

Heirloom dishes served in handcrafted ceramic platters passed along the table, creating natural camaraderie and delight.

Winemaker Dialogue

Featured natural vintners pour rare library magnums and explain the soil geology behind every grape variety.

Midnight Hearth Embers

End the evening with estate herbal infusions, roasted marshmallow chocolates over embers, and warm fireside conversation.

Common Inquiries

Supper Club Etiquette & Questions

Everything you need to know before joining our communal table.

What is the dress code for Supper Club evenings?

We recommend countryside refined: warm layers, sensible footwear for our gravel paths and greenhouse tour, and casual elegance. Ties and formal jackets are never required.

Can dietary restrictions be accommodated at a shared banquet?

Yes. Although dishes are served family-style, our brigade prepares individual portions of gluten-free, vegan, dairy-free, and allergy-sensitive courses alongside the communal service when requested upon reservation.

Can I attend as a solo diner or party of two?

Absolutely. Over half our attendees arrive solo or as couples. The communal table format is designed specifically to make conversation effortless and inviting.
