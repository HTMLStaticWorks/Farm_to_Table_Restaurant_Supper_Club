The Farm-to-Table Ethos

Honoring the Rhythm of Living Soil

Founded on twenty acres of heritage farmland in 2018, Harvest & Hearth was born from an uncompromised devotion to real food. We do not design menus around distant supply chains; our menus are written daily at 6:00 AM after walking our greenhouse rows.

Every cut of dry-aged heritage pork, every heirloom brassica, every drop of cold-pressed sunflower oil, and every loaf of wild-fermented sourdough can be traced directly to our hands or our neighbor farmers within 42 miles.

Communal Dining Guild

The 24-Seat Supper Club Experience

Unlike ordinary restaurant dining, our Supper Club brings twenty-four diners together at a single hand-hewn oak table carved from estate timber.

Over eight family-style courses, the farmers who grew the vegetables and the winemakers who fermented the pairings sit right beside you, sharing the soil stories behind every bite.

  • Alternate Saturday evenings with no table turnovers.
  • 8-Course blind harvest banquet paired with natural cellar reserves.
  • Personalized accommodations for Gluten-Free, Vegan, and Dairy-Free guests.

Pillars of Integrity

Why Our Tables Resonate

Four guiding commitments define every plate, glass, and interaction at Harvest & Hearth.

Certified Regenerative

Cultivating topsoil biology, capturing carbon, and zero synthetic pesticides on all estate grounds.

42-Mile Terroir

Ingredients harvested exclusively within our strict 42-mile regional valley ecosystem.

Ancient Grains

Heirloom wheats stone-milled daily on-site for 48-hour natural sourdough hearth loaves.

Living Wines

Unfiltered low-intervention wines and botanical ciders from biodynamic smallholders.
