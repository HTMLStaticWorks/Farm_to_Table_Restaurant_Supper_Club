The Farm-to-Table Concept

True Nourishment Begins Beneath the Ground

The modern food industry treats ingredients as commodities to be shipped in refrigeration across thousands of miles. At Harvest & Hearth, we believe an ingredient's flavor is indivisible from the health of the soil that fed it.

By practicing regenerative agriculture—planting multi-species cover crops, eliminating deep tillage, and cultivating diverse microbial life—our heritage vegetables contain demonstrably higher mineral density, vibrant natural sugars, and unmistakable vitality.

The 42-Mile Terroir

Our Local Growers & Foragers

Every dish honors the hands that cultivated it. Meet the smallholders, millers, and foragers who feed our dining room.

0.0 Miles (On-Site) Estate Plots

Meadowbrook Organic Plots

Twenty acres of intensive biodynamic cultivation. Produces our daily heirloom Chioggia beets, rainbow chard, purple Romanesco, edible blossoms, and greenhouse microgreens.

Lead Horticulturist: Clara Lindqvist
12 Miles West Ancient Grains

Red Fife Heritage Mill

Family-run water-powered stone mill reviving landrace wheats, ancient emmer, and rye. Milled cold every Tuesday for our 48-hour sourdough loaves and hearth pastries.

Master Miller: Thomas Brennan
18 Miles North Wild Botanicals

North Ridge Wild Foragers

A sustainable guild harvesting wild mountain chanterelles, black pine truffles, spruce tips, wild watercress, and seasonal mountain berries without disturbing forest roots.

Forager Lead: Maya O'Connor
19 Miles East Spring Waters

Clear Creek Fishery

Glacial run-off streams providing sustainable rainbow trout and brook charr. Reared with natural water oxygenation and harvested on day-of-service.

Stream Master: Liam Hayes
22 Miles South Natural Wines

Donkey & Goat Biodynamic

Pioneering natural winemakers producing unfined, unfiltered skin-contact orange wines and field blends farmed according to celestial and soil rhythms.

Vintners: Tracey & Jared Brandt
31 Miles Northwest Pasture Raised

Oak Hill Heritage Farm

Woodland pasture where heritage Berkshire pigs and pasture-raised ducks graze freely under ancient oaks on fallen acorns and heritage Gravenstein apples.

Stewards: The Thorne Family
The Agricultural Cycle

Four Seasons Harvest Calendar

What our land provides across the natural year. Our menus change continuously as crops enter and leave their seasonal peak.

Season Produce & Greens Forest & Foraged Hearth & Cellar Focus
🌱 Spring
March – May		 Green asparagus, wild pea tendrils, French breakfast radishes, baby leeks, young sorrel. Black morel mushrooms, tender stinging nettles, wild wood garlic ramps, spruce buds. Whole wood-fired baby lamb, sheep milk labneh, birch sap reductions, Pet-Nat sparklings.
☀️ Summer
June – August		 Heirloom Sungold tomatoes, sweet bi-color corn, zucchini blossoms, lemon verbena. Wild mountain blackberries, elderflower blossoms, wood sorrel, elderberries. Open flame whole trout, smoked heirloom peppers, raw honey cold treats, orange macerations.
🍂 Autumn
Sept – Nov		 Chioggia beets, honeynut & kabocha squashes, Romanesco, rainbow chard, parsnips. Golden chanterelles, porcini, pine mushrooms, wild mountain cranberries. Oak-roasted heritage pork, aged duck breast, damson plum glazes, skin-contact Malvasia.
❄️ Winter
Dec – Feb		 Cavolo nero, Jerusalem artichokes, purple winter cabbage, greenhouse brassicas. Coastal black truffles, wild juniper berries, dried chanterelles, winter roots. Lacto-fermented cellar preserves, 45-day dry-aged beef, warm sourdough congee, aged Syrah.
