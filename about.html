The Story of Harvest & Hearth

Regenerating Soil, Rekindling Fire

We are not just a dining room; we are twenty acres of working living ecology where every plate begins six months before it is served.

Our Origin

Born from Soil Despair, Healed by Nature

In 2018, when Chef Arlo Vance and Agronomist Maeve Thorne acquired the historic Meadowbrook parcel, the land had been depleted by decades of industrial monoculture. Rather than planting immediately, they spent two full years seeding multi-species clover, winter rye, and radish cover crops to restore the mycorrhizal fungi fungal networks.

Today, the farm hums with biodiversity: heirloom orchards producing 30 varieties of antique apples, heritage chickens foraging pest larvae under solar panels, and greenhouses warmed entirely by compost decomposition tubes.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Path

From an abandoned valley plot to an internationally celebrated agrarian sanctuary.

2018

The First Seeds Planted

Acquired twenty acres in Highland Valley and began intensive cover-cropping and topsoil bioremediation.

2020

The 6-Ton Stone Hearth

Hand-built our signature brick and river-stone hearth with three distinct roasting zones fueled by fallen orchard oak.

2022

Inaugural Supper Club

Launched the 24-seat monthly communal supper club, uniting local growers, artisans, and adventurous diners.

2024

Michelin Green Star Accolade

Honored with the Michelin Green Star for uncompromised zero-waste gastronomy, circular composting, and pure local sourcing.

2026

Heritage Seed Sanctuary

Established a non-profit seed bank safeguarding 180 antique vegetable varieties threatened by climate disruption.
